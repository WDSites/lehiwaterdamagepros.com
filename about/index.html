About Lehi Water Damage Pros

Lehi Water Damage Pros is a dispatch service for water damage restoration and mold remediation in Lehi, Utah and nearby towns. We connect homeowners with licensed and insured restoration professionals, around the clock.

Call (385) 988-9410

How Dispatch Works

When you call, a dispatcher asks what happened, where the water is and how long it has been there. You are told what to shut off and what to leave alone. A restoration professional is then arranged, and same-day response is available when crews are free.

We explain scope and price before work starts. We do not promise arrival times, and we do not state insurance payouts.

What We Cover

Our focus is water damage restoration, from emergency water removal and water mitigation to water damage repair. Mold remediation is a secondary service, because mold often follows a leak.

Where We Work

Home base is Lehi, at Traverse Mountain, and the dispatch covers American Fork, Saratoga Springs, Pleasant Grove, Eagle Mountain and Draper.

Standards We Point To

The published industry standards for this work are ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage restoration and ANSI/IICRC S520 for mold remediation. We encourage you to ask any company whether its crews work to them.

We do not publish license numbers, awards or customer counts. Ask the dispatcher about the company that will be sent to your home.

How We Think About Honesty

We would rather say what we do not know than invent it. That is why this site has no review counts, awards or years in business. We have not published numbers we cannot show, and we do not copy testimonials from other places.

What we can show is how the work is explained: scope and price before work starts, readings during drying and plain answers when you call.

How the Site Is Organized

Each service has its own page, each town we serve has a page, and the blog answers common questions in more detail. Service pages describe what a crew does, and town pages describe local conditions. Blog posts are written for people who are still deciding what to do.

If you want to talk instead of read, the number is in the header on every page. A dispatcher answers day or night.

What We Ask of You

  • Tell us what happened and when it started
  • Tell us whether there is any electrical hazard
  • Take photos before moving items
  • Keep receipts for emergency steps
  • Ask questions about scope and price

Clear information helps the dispatcher choose the right service and helps the crew arrive prepared.

What Happens After You Hang Up

Once the dispatcher has your address and a description, a crew is arranged and you are told what to do while you wait. That usually means closing a valve, moving valuables and staying out of any area near the electrical panel. Same-day response is available when crews are free.

The crew explains scope and price before work starts. If something changes once walls are open, they tell you before adding to the plan.

Why Water Comes First and Mold Comes Second

Most of what we do is water damage work, because water is what starts almost every mold problem. Drying a structure quickly is the most reliable way to avoid mold later. Mold remediation is offered as a secondary service for cases where growth is already present.

That order also shapes this site. The service pages for water come first, and the mold remediation page comes last.

Where to Read More

If you want to understand a service before calling, start with the services index. If you want to see how local conditions change the work, the service area page links to every town we cover.

Everything on this site is written to be useful before you ever pick up the phone.

A Note on What You Will Not Find Here

You will not find invented reviews, made-up star ratings or counts of jobs done. You will also not find a staff biography, because we would rather leave a gap than fill it with something untrue. If we publish a credential in the future, it will be one we can show.

The industry standards we mention, ANSI/IICRC S500 and S520, are public documents. Naming them helps you ask better questions of any company you call.

Questions We Hear Most on the First Call

People ask whether the water can wait until morning, whether they should keep the power on and whether their insurer will pay. The honest answers are that standing water should not wait, that power near water should be off until checked, and that we cannot predict what an insurer decides.

We can tell you what documents help a claim, such as dated photos and receipts, and we explain our own scope in plain terms.
