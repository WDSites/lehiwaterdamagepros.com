If Water Is Still Spreading

Five Things To Do Before the Crew Arrives

These steps limit damage while you wait. None of them replace a call, and none are worth risking an electrical shock.

Stop the source

Close the main water valve, or the valve at the fixture. If the water came from a sprinkler or irrigation line, shut that supply as well.

Mind the electricity

Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without standing in water. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911 first.

Lift what you can

Move small furniture, rugs, books and electronics off wet floors. Place foil or wood blocks under furniture legs on wet carpet.

Keep people and pets out

Water from a sewer drain, a toilet backup or outdoor flooding can carry bacteria. Treat it as contaminated and do not wade through it.

Photograph before you clean

Take wide and close photos of every wet room and damaged item. Your insurer will ask for them, and crews use them to confirm scope.

Local Causes

Why Lehi Homes Leak, Flood and Stay Wet

Lehi has grown fast. The Census Bureau puts the city at 95,313 residents in its July 2025 estimate, and 75.2 percent of housing units are owner-occupied. Newer homes in neighborhoods around Traverse Mountain rely on supply lines, appliance hoses and finished basements, and those are the parts that fail.

That matters because a hidden failure behind a finished basement wall can run for weeks before a stain shows. Moisture readings find it faster than the eye does.

Older Lehi buildings fail differently. The Lehi Main Street Historic District, listed on the National Register, holds brick buildings from the 1890s through the 1920s, many with limestone foundations. Old masonry and old plumbing take water slowly and release it slowly, so drying plans for those buildings run longer than for a 2015 townhome.

If you own or rent downtown, tell the crew the building's age on the first call. It changes the equipment and the drying schedule.

Irrigation season adds a Lehi-specific cause. The city's conservation page lists the pressurized irrigation season as April 15 through October 15 under Ordinance 11-2022. A broken irrigation lateral, a failed sprinkler head or a pushed-in window well can send water toward a foundation during those months.

Say whether the water came from irrigation or from the house supply. A crew handles the two sources with different caution.

Lehi Water and Permit Facts

The Lehi City Water Department manages culinary water, pressurized irrigation, sewer and storm drains, each through its own division. Knowing which system a leak belongs to tells you whom to call about the shut-off and who is responsible for the line.

For repairs that need permits, the city's Building and Inspections department takes applications through its online portal and asks for 24 hours notice before an inspection. Permit rules vary by job, so ask the department whether your repair needs one before the drywall goes back up.

Which Kind of Water Are You Dealing With?

The industry standard for water damage restoration, ANSI/IICRC S500, sorts water by how contaminated it is. The category decides what can be dried in place and what has to be removed. Ask any company you hire whether its crews work to that standard.

Water categories at a glance
CategoryTypical sourceWhat usually happens
Category 1Broken supply line, tub overflow, rain through a clean roofExtract and dry in place. Most materials can be saved if the response is fast.
Category 2Washing machine or dishwasher discharge, a toilet overflow with urine onlyExtract, remove carpet pad and other porous materials, clean and dry the rest.
Category 3Sewage backup, river or groundwater flooding, long-standing waterContain, remove affected porous materials, disinfect, then dry and rebuild.

Clean water can move to a higher category if it sits. That is one reason hours matter.

Insurance and Your Claim

Water damage coverage depends on the cause. Sudden and accidental losses, like a burst pipe, are often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not. Flood damage is usually excluded from a standard homeowners policy and needs separate flood coverage. The Utah Insurance Department says the same about flood and notes that flood policies usually carry a 30-day waiting period.

Check your policy or ask your insurer before you assume anything. We never promise a claim will be approved and we do not state payouts.

What to document for your claim

  • Wide and close photos of every wet room, taken before cleanup
  • The time you found the water and the source if you know it
  • A list of damaged belongings with approximate age and value
  • The moisture readings and scope of work from the restoration crew
  • Receipts for emergency steps such as a plumber or hotel stay
