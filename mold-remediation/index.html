Mold Remediation in Lehi, UT

Mold remediation in Lehi, UT starts with fixing the moisture, because mold only grows where water is present. A crew then contains the area, removes affected materials, cleans and dries the space, and checks the result. Mold work is secondary to water damage on this site, but a large share of calls start with a hidden leak.

A Musty Smell, a Dark Spot, or a Wall That Never Quite Dries

Most people do not search for mold remediation. They notice a dark spot in the corner of a shower ceiling, a smell in a closet, or a stain that returns after painting. They are describing the same problem. Moisture stayed in a building material long enough for mold to start.

The first question is where the water came from. A toilet supply line, a leaking sink, an air conditioner condensate line, a roof or siding leak and a basement window well are the usual sources. Until the source is fixed, no cleaning lasts.

How Remediation Works

1

Find and fix the source

A plumber, roofer or HVAC technician repairs the leak first.

2

Contain

Plastic barriers and negative air keep spores from moving to clean rooms.

3

Remove and clean

Affected porous materials come out, and surfaces are cleaned with HEPA vacuums and damp wipes.

4

Dry and verify

The area is dried and checked, and clearance testing can confirm the result.

The published industry standard for mold remediation is ANSI/IICRC S520. Ask any company whether its crews work to it, and ask what happens if testing finds the area is not clear.

Mold Removal and Black Mold Cleanup

Color does not tell you what a mold is, and it does not tell you how serious it is. Black mold, green mold and white growth all call for the same response: find the moisture, contain, remove, dry. A person with health concerns should talk to a physician.

Small surface growth on a hard, non-porous surface can often be cleaned. Growth on drywall, insulation or carpet usually means removal, because mold roots into porous materials.

Mold After Water Damage

Mold can begin within a day or two of a wet event, and it is more likely when wet materials stay wet. That is why fast drying matters. If a leak was found late, a wall may already have growth behind it.

Start with water mitigation if the structure is still wet. After removal, water damage repair rebuilds.

Mold Clearance for a Home Sale

Some buyers' lenders ask for mold to be addressed before a loan closes. Clearance testing by an independent party can document that an area is clear. Ask your agent and lender what they will accept.
