How to Choose When More Than One Service Fits

Many jobs involve more than one service. A burst pipe starts with emergency removal, moves to mitigation and drying, and ends with repair. When you call, describe what happened and what you see, and the dispatcher will help you find the right starting point.

If you are not sure whether you have a water problem or a mold problem, start with water. Mold needs moisture, so drying and finding the source usually come first.

What Every Service Has in Common

  • Scope and price are explained before work starts
  • Moisture is measured and recorded during drying
  • Damaged materials are removed only when they cannot be dried
  • Photos and notes support your insurance conversation
  • Dispatch is open 24/7 from Lehi

We do not promise an arrival time or an insurance result. We do explain what we see and what each step involves, in plain language.
