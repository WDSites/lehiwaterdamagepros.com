Appliance Leak Water Damage in Lehi, UT

Appliance leak water damage in Lehi, UT often starts small and goes unseen. A washer hose, a dishwasher seal or a refrigerator ice-maker line can wet a floor for days. This page explains where those leaks hide, what to do first and how a crew dries them.

Washing Machines and Dishwashers

A washer supply hose carries full pressure whenever the faucets are open. Braided hoses can fail at the crimped ends, and rubber hoses bulge before they burst. A drain hose that slips off the standpipe sends gray water to the floor during every spin.

A dishwasher usually leaks at the door gasket, the inlet valve or the drain connection. The water goes under the cabinet toe-kick and into the subfloor, so the kitchen looks fine while the cabinet base stays wet.

Refrigerator Lines and Hidden Leaks

A refrigerator ice-maker or water-dispenser line is a thin tube with a small saddle valve or a fitting. When it fails it drips behind the unit, often for weeks. The first sign is usually a soft spot in the flooring or a sour smell from behind the fridge.

Other hidden sources include a water softener, a humidifier tied into the furnace and a utility sink drain. Each can leak inside a cabinet or closet where a slow leak is hard to spot.

Safety Steps: When an Appliance Is Leaking

  1. Close the shutoff valve to the appliance, or the main if the valve is stuck.
  2. Unplug the appliance only if the floor around the outlet is dry.
  3. Turn off the breaker for the room if water reached outlets or the panel.
  4. Pull the appliance away from the wall if you can do it without standing in water.
  5. Take photos of the hose, the valve and the floor before cleaning.

Do not run a household vacuum on water. A wet-dry vacuum rated for liquid is safer.

How a Crew Dries an Appliance Leak

1

Extract

Wet vacuums remove water from the floor and toe-kick area.

2

Find the path

A moisture meter and thermal camera follow the water under cabinets, along walls and through floors.

3

Open when needed

Toe-kicks, baseboard and lower board come off if the cavity is wet.

4

Dry and verify

Air movers and dehumidifiers run, and readings are logged until they match dry reference material.

ANSI/IICRC S500, a published industry standard, sorts water into categories by contamination. That matters for a dishwasher or washer drain.

Insurance and Appliance Leaks

A sudden hose failure is often treated differently from a leak that dripped for months. Policies often exclude damage that built up over a long time. Read the language about sudden and accidental discharge, and ask your insurer how it handles appliance leaks.

The Utah Insurance Department asks you to call your insurer quickly, take photos or video and hold permanent repairs until the insurer has inspected.

Prevention and Mistakes to Avoid

  • Replace rubber washer hoses with braided ones and check them each year
  • Leave a few inches behind the washer and fridge so you can see a drip
  • Shut the washer valves when you leave on a trip
  • Do not cover a wet floor with a rug and hope it dries
  • Do not wait to call after a leak under a cabinet
