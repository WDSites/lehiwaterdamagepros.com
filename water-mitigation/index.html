Emergency Water Mitigation in Lehi, UT

Water mitigation in Lehi means limiting the damage in the first 24 to 72 hours: stopping the source, pulling water out, mapping moisture and setting drying equipment before the structure fails. Dispatch is open 24/7. If you found water a few minutes ago, shut the main valve and call now.

My Wall Is Wet, Now What?

You may not call it mitigation. You may say that a ceiling is dripping, a wall feels cold and damp, or the carpet squishes. Those are mitigation problems. Mitigation is the trade name for stopping the damage from growing while the building is still wet.

It starts with the source, moves to extraction, then to drying. It is not repair. Repair comes later, once the structure reads dry, and you can read about it on our water damage repair page.

When a Leak Is an Emergency

Treat any active leak, any water near the electrical panel and any sewage as an emergency. The line is open 24/7. Call, describe what you see, and a restoration professional tells you what to shut off. A same-day crew is possible when one is free, but we do not promise a time of arrival.

While you wait, close the main valve, move valuables, and turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and easy to reach. If water is near outlets, stay out and call the utility or 911 first.

Structural Drying: Why Equipment Matters

Air that moves is only half of drying. Drying works when wet materials release moisture into the air and a dehumidifier removes it, so the room stays dry enough to keep pulling moisture out. Air movers set the flow, and low-grain dehumidifiers manage the humidity.

In a wall cavity or under a floor, crews drill small holes or lift baseboards to push air where the water is. Moisture readings at the start, daily and at the end decide when equipment leaves. Callers sometimes ask about renting an air mover. Drying without readings is a guess, which is why mitigation is a service, not a rental.

How long does it take? Many jobs run several days. A small carpeted bedroom dries faster than a finished basement with wet walls, and Category 3 water adds removal before drying starts.

Mitigation Versus Repair: Where One Ends and the Other Begins

Mitigation limits further damage. It covers shutting off water, extraction, removal of materials that cannot dry and the drying itself. Repair puts the building back, with drywall, paint, flooring and trim.

Keeping the two separate in your scope and invoice makes insurance conversations easier. It also lets you compare quotes for each stage on equal terms.

Daily Monitoring Is Part of the Job

Drying is not a set-it-and-leave job. A crew returns to read moisture, adjust equipment and decide whether a wall needs to be opened. The readings are recorded so the end of drying is a number, not an opinion.

Ask how often visits happen and who answers questions in between. A plan with clear check-ins is easier to trust.

Lehi Context for Drying

Lehi's Main Street Historic District, listed on the National Register, contains brick buildings from the 1890s through the 1920s, many with limestone foundations. The Lehi Roller Mills at 700 East Main Street, built in 1905 and 1906, has brick walls on a stone foundation.

Masonry and stone take on water slowly and give it up slowly, so a drying plan for a building like that runs longer than one for a newer drywall home. Tell us the age and the materials on the first call.
